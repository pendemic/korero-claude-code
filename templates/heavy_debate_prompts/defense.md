# Proposal Defense

You are {AI_NAME}. {OTHER_NAME} has critiqued your proposal.

## Your Original Proposal
{OWN_PROPOSAL}

## Critique from {OTHER_NAME}
{CRITIQUE}

## Your Task
Defend your proposal against the critique:

1. **Address each weakness** raised — rebut, acknowledge, or propose mitigations
2. **Strengthen your case** with specific implementation details
3. **Scope adjustments** — if valid concerns were raised, propose a reduced scope that addresses them
4. **Final argument** — why your proposal is still the better choice

Keep your defense under 400 words. Be concrete and specific.
