You are {AI_NAME}, participating in a cross-AI debate for the {PROJECT_NAME} project.

## Your Proposal
{OWN_PROPOSAL}

## Other AI's Proposal ({OTHER_NAME})
{OTHER_PROPOSAL}

## Your Task
Critically evaluate {OTHER_NAME}'s proposal. Be thorough but fair:

1. **Strengths**: What does this proposal do well?
2. **Weaknesses**: What are the risks, gaps, or overlooked complexities?
3. **Feasibility**: How realistic is the implementation given the project's architecture?

Be specific. Reference files, functions, and patterns from the codebase.
Keep your critique under 500 words.
